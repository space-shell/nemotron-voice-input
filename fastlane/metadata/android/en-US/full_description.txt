Nemotron Voice Input turns your speech into text entirely on your device. Words appear live while you speak — no internet connection, no account, no tracking. Your voice never leaves your phone.

THREE WAYS TO USE IT
• Voice-input popup: tap the microphone on a compatible keyboard (such as Microsoft SwiftKey) or a website's voice-search button, and a compact panel transcribes live over the current app.
• System speech service: the app registers as your device's speech-to-text provider, streaming partial results to apps that use SpeechRecognizer.
• Voice keyboard: a dedicated streaming keyboard of its own. Keyboards that switch to voice IMEs (such as FUTO Keyboard) jump straight to it from their mic key.

LIVE TEXT AS YOU SPEAK
Text is committed to the field word by word as it firms up, with the still-uncertain tail refined as you keep talking — not a record-then-wait-and-paste flow. After dictation the keyboard hands control back to the keyboard you were using before (optional, on by default), and can run as a movable floating panel.

WORKS OFFLINE, ALWAYS
After a one-time download of the NVIDIA Nemotron speech streaming model (English, ~700 MB) on first launch, everything runs fully on-device with constant memory — dictation length is not limited, and no audio ever leaves your phone. The only permissions the app needs are the microphone and network access for that first download.

ALSO INCLUDED
• Transcribe audio files: share an audio file with the app to turn a recording into text
• Hold-to-cancel: long-press to throw away a dictation
• Material 3 design with dynamic color, light/dark themes, and a built-in speed benchmark

POWERED BY OPEN SOURCE
Built as a fork of notune/android_transcribe_app (MIT), with a Rust core running transcription through transcribe.cpp. The model is nvidia/nemotron-speech-streaming-en-0.6b (NVIDIA Open Model License), downloaded on first launch as a Q8_0 GGUF and verified against a pinned checksum.
